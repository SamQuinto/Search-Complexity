# Study 3 · Prolific experiment

Study 3 compares two versions of a three-person office-sharing negotiation. Each version has three roles and nine Include/Exclude decisions. The conditions use identical role narratives, the same bargaining-zone share, and different payoff tables.

The study's Empirica setup uses the low- and high-search-complexity JSONs as the two treatment materials. The JSONs contain private role and payoff information and are held in the study's setup folder. The app assigns one participant to each role and records the conditions, complete offers, and decisions. Agreement requires all three participants to accept the same offer and receive more than zero points from it.
